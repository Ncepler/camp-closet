# Product Context — Another Summer

## Brand name
Another Summer

## Tagline
Camp gear, second chapter.

## Register
brand (marketing surfaces, landing pages, camp browse pages) + product (seller dashboard, submit flow, checkout, admin)

## Product purpose
A peer-to-peer resale marketplace for used summer camp clothing. Organized by camp. Every listing reviewed by a human before going live. The price you see is the price you pay — shipping included. Buyers and sellers never interact directly.

## Users

**Buyers:** Parents of current campers who want affordable branded gear for their kid. They trust this platform because every listing is curated, prices are fixed, and there are no surprises. They are not bargain hunters — they are practical families who feel good about sustainability.

**Sellers:** Families whose kids have aged out of a camp or grown out of their gear. They want a simple way to pass items along to someone who will use them. Low friction to list, and a trustworthy payout.

**Admins:** One or two operators running the marketplace. They approve listings, monitor orders, follow up on shipping, and handle disputes.

## Core values
1. Sustainability first — every transaction is an environmental act
2. Curated quality — everything is reviewed; no junk goes live
3. Transparent and fair — fixed prices, no haggling, no surprises
4. Trust — admin in the middle; buyers and sellers never meet

## Tone
Confident and warm. Not salesy. Not preachy about sustainability — matter-of-fact. The brand assumes the buyer already gets it; it doesn't need to convince them. Reads like a trusted parent friend, not a startup.

## Anti-references (do not look like these)
- Generic Shopify store (templated, impersonal)
- Fast fashion e-commerce (loud CTAs, countdown timers, fake urgency)
- Hippie/crunchy sustainability sites (hemp textures, hand-drawn fonts, excessive earth tones)
- Any site that relies on stock photography of smiling families

## Strategic principles
- One price per item type — no negotiation, no quality tiers
- Items never disappear at 0 stock — waitlist instead
- Nothing goes live without admin review
- Sustainability stats are contextual, not decorative
